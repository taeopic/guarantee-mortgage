What makes the Low Down Payment Loan different.

Low down payment programs come with trade-offs (PMI, slightly higher rates) but the right structure makes them an excellent choice for many buyers.

Is this loan right for you?

Low down payment programs work when your monthly cash flow is healthy but your savings are thinner.

  • You have at least 3% to 5% of the purchase price available for down payment
  • Your credit score is 620 or higher
  • Your DTI is under 45% on the new mortgage payment
  • You can absorb a monthly PMI charge of $50 to $200 per $100K of loan
  • You want to preserve cash reserves for moving, furniture, and emergency fund
  • You are open to PMI for the first few years and a refinance later when you have equity

Common Questions

What borrowers ask most.

Is conventional 97 better than FHA?
It depends. Conventional 97 PMI drops at 20% equity. FHA MIP often lasts the life of the loan. We run both so the actual monthly payment, not the headline rate, drives the decision.
Do I have to pay PMI forever?
On conventional loans, no. PMI removes at 80% LTV (request) or 78% LTV (automatic). On FHA, MIP typically lasts the life of the loan unless you put 10%+ down.
Can I combine a low-down loan with DPA?
Yes. Texas DPA programs stack on top of conventional and FHA low-down programs. We coordinate the whole package.
How much will PMI cost me per month?
Typically $50 to $200 per $100K of loan, depending on credit, LTV, and program. We disclose the exact PMI factor in your pre-qual.
Should I just save up for 20% down instead?
Often no. If home prices rise faster than your savings, waiting can cost more than PMI. We model both scenarios so you decide with real numbers.
