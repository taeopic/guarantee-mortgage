Is this loan right for you?

Investor loans suit borrowers with capital and a strategy. Here is who closes cleanest.

  • You have 15 to 25% available for down payment plus reserves
  • Your credit score is 680 or higher
  • Your existing rental portfolio shows positive cash flow (or you are buying a cash-flowing first property)
  • You have 6+ months of PITI reserves on the new loan plus reserves on existing rentals
  • You have a clear hold strategy (long-term rental, short-term rental, or flip-then-rent)
  • Your DTI either fits conventional guidelines or you are using a DSCR loan to bypass DTI

Common Questions

What borrowers ask most.

How much down payment do I need on an investment property?
Typically 15% to 25%. Conventional caps at 25% down for the best pricing. DSCR programs often allow 20%.
Can I qualify based on the rental income?
Yes, through a DSCR loan. The property's projected or actual rent qualifies the loan with no personal income verification.
Can I finance more than 4 properties?
Yes. Fannie Mae allows up to 10 financed properties per borrower with the right reserves. Beyond 10, we use portfolio lenders.
Do investment loans cost more than primary residence loans?
Yes. Expect rates 0.5% to 1% higher than primary, plus larger down payment, plus more reserves required.
Can I use a 1031 exchange with these loans?
Yes. We coordinate with your qualified intermediary to align the loan funding with the 1031 timeline.
