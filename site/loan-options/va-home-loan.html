What makes the VA Loan different.

VA loans waive private mortgage insurance, allow seller-paid closing costs up to 4%, and let you reuse the benefit your whole life.

Is this loan right for you?

Use this honest checklist before applying. If most of these describe you, the VA program will likely be your best path.

  • You served on active duty 90+ continuous days during wartime or 181+ days during peacetime
  • You served 6+ years in the National Guard or Reserves, or qualify as a surviving spouse
  • You plan to occupy the home as your primary residence within 60 days of closing
  • Your credit score is 580 or higher and your debt-to-income ratio is under 41%
  • You have a valid Certificate of Eligibility or are willing to let us pull one for you at no cost

Common Questions

What borrowers ask most.

Do I have to be a first-time buyer to use the VA loan?
No. You can use VA benefits multiple times throughout your life. As long as you have remaining entitlement or restore it by paying off the prior loan, the program is available again.
Is there really no down payment required?
Correct. On VA purchase loans up to county loan limits, you can put 0% down. Above the county limit you contribute 25% of the amount over the limit.
What is the VA funding fee?
A one-time fee that helps fund the program. It ranges from 1.25% to 3.3% depending on service category, down payment, and whether it is your first use. It can be financed into the loan.
Can my spouse use my VA benefit?
A surviving spouse of a service member who died in the line of duty or from a service-connected disability may qualify. Married couples can also use a Joint VA Loan.
Can I use a VA loan for an investment property?
No. The home must be your primary residence and you must occupy it within 60 days. You can later move out and keep the property as a rental once you have lived in it.
