VA Home Loan

Service earned. Zero down. No PMI.

Active duty, veterans, and qualifying spouses. We have processed hundreds of VA loans across Texas including in the Aggieland and Houston military communities.

Program Highlights

What sets this loan apart.

  • 0% down on primary residences up to county loan limits
  • No private mortgage insurance, ever
  • Funding fee can be financed into the loan
  • Streamline IRRRL available for existing VA borrowers

Who is this loan for?

If any of these benefits speak to your situation, a Guarantee Mortgage loan officer can run a no-cost scenario in under 24 hours showing your exact rate, payment, and closing costs. We are licensed in Texas and serve every county in the state from our College Station and Mont Belvieu offices.

What documents will I need?

For a pre-approval, we typically need two recent pay stubs, two years of W-2s (or tax returns if self-employed), two months of bank statements, and a copy of your driver license. Self-employed and investor borrowers may need a few additional documents which your loan officer will spell out clearly upfront.
