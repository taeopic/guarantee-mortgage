Why this loan

What makes the Cash-Out Refi different.

Cash-out refinancing replaces your existing mortgage with a larger one and gives you the difference in cash at closing.

1
Lower rates than HELOCsCash-out refi rates are fixed and typically lower than variable HELOC rates, especially during rising-rate environments.
2
Lump sum at closingReceive the cash-out amount in a single wire on closing day. Use it as you choose.
3
Up to 80% LTV on primary residencePull up to 80% of your appraised value, minus any existing liens, on a primary residence.
4
Tax-deductible interest (when used for home improvements)Interest on cash-out funds used to substantially improve the home may be tax-deductible. Consult your CPA.
5
Consolidate high-interest debtRoll credit cards, personal loans, and other high-rate debt into a single lower mortgage payment.
6
Fund major renovationsKitchen, bath, addition, pool. Increase your home's value while improving day-to-day life.
7
Pay for education or major expensesTuition, medical, business launch. Tap home equity at mortgage rates instead of credit-card rates.
8
Fixed payment for the life of the loanLock in a fixed rate and a fixed monthly payment. No surprises.

Is this loan right for you?

Cash-out is right when the rate, the use, and your equity all line up. Use this checklist.

  • You have at least 20% equity in your primary residence (15% for some investor programs)
  • Your credit score is 620 or higher
  • Your DTI after the new loan is under 43%
  • You have a clear, productive use for the funds (renovations, debt payoff, major expense)
  • You are comfortable converting unsecured debt into mortgage debt for the lower rate
  • You plan to stay in the home long enough to amortize closing costs

Common Questions

What borrowers ask most.

How much cash can I take out?
Up to 80% of your home's appraised value minus any existing mortgage balance on a primary residence. Investment properties cap at 70 to 75% LTV.
Is the interest tax-deductible?
Interest on cash-out funds used to substantially improve the home is typically deductible. Funds used for other purposes are generally not. Confirm with your CPA.
Cash-out refi vs HELOC, which is better?
Cash-out refi locks a fixed rate on the full amount. HELOC is variable and revolving. Most clients prefer cash-out when rates are stable and they need a lump sum.
Will my monthly payment go up?
Usually yes, because the loan amount is larger. We model the new payment upfront so there are no surprises.
Can I do a cash-out on an investment property?
Yes. LTV caps are tighter (typically 70 to 75%) and rates are slightly higher, but the program exists and we run it regularly.
