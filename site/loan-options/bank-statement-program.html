Is this loan right for you?

Bank statement loans fit specific borrower profiles. Use this checklist.

  • You have been self-employed in the same line of work for at least 2 years
  • Your bank deposits show consistent business or personal cash flow
  • Your tax returns understate your real income due to legitimate business write-offs
  • Your credit score is 660 or higher
  • You have 10 to 15% available for down payment plus reserves
  • You can supply 12 or 24 months of clean bank statements (no large unexplained deposits)

Common Questions

What borrowers ask most.

How do bank statement loans calculate my income?
Most programs average 12 to 24 months of deposits, then apply an expense factor (typically 50% for business accounts, less for personal). The result is your qualifying income.
What counts as a deposit?
Business revenue, customer payments, transfers from other business accounts. Personal transfers, refunds, and one-time large deposits typically do not count.
How is this different from a stated-income loan?
Stated-income loans (pre-2008) required no documentation at all. Bank statement loans require actual deposit documentation, and underwriting verifies them.
Can I use personal bank statements?
Yes, if you pay yourself regularly from the business. Some programs prefer personal accounts; others prefer business. We match to the right program.
What is the rate on bank statement loans?
Typically 1% to 2% above conventional. The flexibility on income docs is the trade-off.
