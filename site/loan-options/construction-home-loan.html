Is this loan right for you?

Construction loans suit borrowers building from the ground up, not buying existing inventory.

  • You have selected a licensed builder with experience and clean references
  • Your credit score is 680 or higher
  • You have 10% to 20% in down payment (including land equity if applicable)
  • Your DTI is under 43% on the projected permanent payment
  • Your builder can provide a complete budget, plans, and build timeline
  • You can carry your current housing cost plus the interest payments during the build

Common Questions

What borrowers ask most.

What is a one-time-close construction loan?
A loan that funds both the construction phase and the permanent mortgage in a single closing. You sign once, you pay one set of closing costs, and you lock your permanent rate upfront.
How long does the construction phase last?
Typically 9 to 12 months. The program allows up to 12 to 18 months in most cases, with extensions available if needed.
Can I act as my own builder?
Most institutional construction lenders require a licensed builder with a track record. Owner-builder loans exist but are rare.
What happens if construction goes over budget?
We build a contingency reserve (typically 10%) into the original budget. Overruns beyond that require additional borrower contribution or scope reduction.
Do I make payments during construction?
Yes, interest-only on the funds drawn so far. The payment grows as draws happen, then converts to full P&I at conversion.
