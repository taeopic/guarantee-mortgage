Is this loan right for you?

Jumbo borrowers usually have specific scenarios. Here are the profiles that close cleanest.

  • Your purchase price puts your loan above the conforming limit (currently $806,500 in most Texas counties)
  • Your credit score is 700 or higher and your DTI is under 43%
  • You have 2+ months of PITI in reserves after closing, ideally 6+ for the strongest pricing
  • Your income is documentable through tax returns, W-2s, or verified business cash flow
  • You are buying a primary residence, vacation home, or high-end investment in Texas
  • You may be a self-employed professional or business owner with consistent multi-year income

Common Questions

What borrowers ask most.

What is the conforming loan limit in Texas in 2026?
$806,500 in most counties. High-cost counties around major metros can go higher. Any loan amount above that ceiling is classified as jumbo.
Do jumbo loans always have higher rates?
Not anymore. In recent years jumbo pricing has often beaten conforming, particularly on the strongest credit and down-payment profiles. We shop both whenever a borrower is near the line.
How much down payment do I need on a jumbo loan?
10% to 25% depending on the investor and your file. Most files settle around 15% to 20% down with the best pricing tier kicking in at 25%.
Can I get a jumbo loan as a self-employed borrower?
Yes. Tax-return jumbos, bank-statement jumbos, and asset-depletion jumbos all exist. We match your income story to the right program.
Are jumbo loans available for second homes?
Yes. Second-home jumbos typically require 15% to 20% down and slightly tighter reserves than primary jumbos. We finance vacation homes on the coast and in the Hill Country regularly.
