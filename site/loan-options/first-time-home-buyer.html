Is this loan right for you?

If most of these describe you, the first-time buyer path is built for your situation.

  • You have not owned a home in the last three years (federal definition of first-time buyer)
  • Your household income is at or below the area median income for your county
  • You have stable employment and a credit score of at least 580 (FHA) or 620 (conventional)
  • Your savings are limited but your monthly cash flow can comfortably absorb a mortgage payment
  • You are open to homebuyer education to unlock additional grant and rate programs

Common Questions

What borrowers ask most.

What counts as a first-time buyer?
Federally, anyone who has not owned a primary residence in the last three years. You can still qualify even if you previously owned a home decades ago.
How much do I really need for a down payment?
As little as 0% (VA, USDA), 3% (Conventional 97), or 3.5% (FHA) of the purchase price. Many first-time buyers close with under $5,000 out of pocket once you factor in seller concessions and DPA.
What is down payment assistance and do I qualify?
DPA programs are grants or second-lien loans from state and city agencies that cover some or all of your down payment. Eligibility is income-based and varies by program. We check every program for you.
How long does the whole process take?
From application to keys, typically 30 to 45 days. The home search itself often takes longer than the loan.
Do I need to take a homebuyer education course?
Required for some DPA programs and recommended for everyone. The course is online, takes about 6 hours, and often unlocks a rate or fee discount.
