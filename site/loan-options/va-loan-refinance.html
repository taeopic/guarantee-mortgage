Why this loan

What makes the VA IRRRL different.

The IRRRL exists for one reason: to make it brutally easy for VA borrowers to lower their rate. Take advantage of it.

1
No appraisal required (most cases)Skip the appraisal fee and the appraisal contingency. Your home's value is presumed.
2
No income re-verificationWe do not need new pay stubs, W-2s, or tax returns in most cases. Your VA history qualifies you.
3
Reduced funding feeVA IRRRL funding fee is just 0.5% versus 2.15% to 3.3% on a new purchase. Big savings on closing costs.
4
Roll closing costs into the loanBring $0 to the table. Closing costs and the funding fee can both be financed into the new loan.
5
Switch from ARM to fixedConvert an existing VA adjustable-rate mortgage to a fixed rate for the rest of your loan's life.
6
Lower monthly paymentThe whole point. Any IRRRL has to make financial sense for the borrower per VA rules.
7
Fastest close in mortgageMost IRRRLs close in under 25 days. We have closed them in 14 when the file is clean.
8
Spouse no longer needs to qualifyIf you originally co-borrowed with a spouse, an IRRRL can remove them from the loan without a full requalification.

Is this loan right for you?

If you already have a VA loan and rates have moved, the IRRRL is almost always worth a 10-minute conversation.

  • You currently have a VA loan in good standing (no 30-day lates in the last 12 months)
  • Current market rates are at least 0.5% lower than your existing rate
  • You plan to keep the home long enough for the savings to outpace the costs
  • You want to move from an adjustable to a fixed rate
  • You want a faster, lower-paperwork refinance than a conventional rate-and-term offers

Common Questions

What borrowers ask most.

Do I need an appraisal for an IRRRL?
No, in most cases. The VA presumes the property still meets its standards. Exception: if you are rolling significant closing costs in and the loan balance grows substantially, an appraisal may be required.
How much can I save with an IRRRL?
The VA requires that your new payment is meaningfully lower (unless converting from ARM to fixed). Most borrowers save $100 to $400 a month at current rate differentials.
Can I take cash out with an IRRRL?
No. The IRRRL is rate-and-term only. For a cash-out VA refinance, you'd use a VA Cash-Out Refinance which has different rules and a full appraisal.
What is the VA IRRRL funding fee?
0.5% of the loan amount, financeable into the loan. Borrowers with 10%+ service-connected disability are exempt.
How fast can an IRRRL close?
We routinely close in 18 to 25 days. Clean files with no service-connected complications have closed in 14.
