Is this loan right for you?

DSCR works for investors whose personal tax return understates their true earning power.

  • You are buying or refinancing an investment property in your name or your LLC
  • Your personal tax returns show low taxable income due to write-offs, depreciation, or business losses
  • The property's projected rent equals or exceeds the principal, interest, taxes, and insurance payment
  • Your credit score is 680 or higher
  • You have 20 to 25% available for down payment plus 3 to 6 months reserves
  • You want to scale beyond Fannie/Freddie's 10-property cap

Common Questions

What borrowers ask most.

What is DSCR?
Debt-Service Coverage Ratio is gross monthly rent divided by monthly PITI. A 1.0 DSCR means rent exactly covers the mortgage. Most programs want 1.0+.
Do you really not need tax returns?
Correct. DSCR programs underwrite to the property's cash flow, not your personal income. Tax returns are not requested.
Can I use projected STR income?
Yes, on some programs. We use AirDNA or similar data to project Airbnb/VRBO revenue and qualify the property.
Can I close in an LLC?
Yes. Most DSCR programs require or prefer LLC ownership for asset protection and tax planning.
How does the rate compare to conventional?
DSCR rates run 0.75% to 1.5% above conventional investment property rates. The flexibility is the trade-off.
