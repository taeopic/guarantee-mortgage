Why this loan

What makes the USDA Loan different.

USDA is the best-kept secret in mortgage. Zero down, no PMI in the traditional sense, and most of rural Texas qualifies.

Roll closing costs into the loanWhen the appraisal supports it, you can finance closing costs and a small amount above purchase price.

Is this loan right for you?

USDA is location-dependent. If your target area qualifies and your income fits the cap, this is hard to beat.

  • The property is in a USDA-eligible area (we verify by address)
  • Your household income is at or below 115% of the area median income for the county
  • Your credit score is at least 640
  • You plan to occupy the home as your primary residence
  • You are a U.S. citizen, permanent resident, or qualified non-citizen
  • Your DTI is under 41% (slightly flexible with compensating factors)

Common Questions

What borrowers ask most.

Is my property in a USDA-eligible area?
Most of rural and small-town Texas is eligible. Check usda.gov's eligibility map or send us the address and we will confirm in minutes.
Is there an income limit?
Yes. Household income must be at or below 115% of the area median income. The cap varies by county and household size. We confirm yours upfront.
Do I have to be a first-time buyer?
No. USDA is open to repeat buyers as long as you do not currently own another adequate primary residence within commuting distance.
Are there acreage limits?
USDA evaluates the site relative to the value of the home. The site cannot account for an outsized portion of total value, and the property cannot be income-producing as a farm.
What is the USDA guarantee fee?
1% upfront (financeable) plus 0.35% annual paid monthly. Lower lifetime cost than FHA's mortgage insurance for most borrowers.
