What makes the FHA Loan different.

FHA is forgiving where other programs are strict. Lower scores, smaller down payment, gift funds welcome, even with some past credit hiccups.

1
3.5% down with 580+ FICOIf your credit score is at least 580, your minimum down payment is just 3.5% of the purchase price.
2
500 to 579 FICO with 10% downEven buyers with lower scores can qualify if they can put 10% down. Few other programs offer this flexibility.

Forgiving on past credit issuesMost lenders allow FHA approval 2 years after a Chapter 7 bankruptcy and 3 years after a foreclosure or short sale.

8
Assumable to future buyersA future buyer can take over your FHA loan at your original rate, a major asset when you sell into a higher-rate market.

Is this loan right for you?

FHA is built for real borrowers with real life circumstances. Here is who tends to win with the program.

  • Your credit score is between 580 and 700 and conventional programs are pricing you uncompetitively
  • You have less than 5% saved for a down payment and want to keep cash reserves intact
  • You are a first-time buyer, or have not owned a home in the last three years
  • Your debt-to-income ratio is in the 40 to 50 percent range
  • You have a past bankruptcy, foreclosure, or short sale that is now 2 to 3 years behind you
  • You are receiving down payment help from family, a state program, or your employer

Common Questions

What borrowers ask most.

Do I have to be a first-time buyer to use an FHA loan?
No. While many first-time buyers choose FHA, the program is open to repeat buyers too. The only restriction is that the home must be your primary residence.
What is FHA mortgage insurance?
FHA loans carry an upfront mortgage insurance premium (1.75% of the loan, financeable) and an annual premium paid monthly. The annual premium typically stays for the life of the loan unless you put 10% or more down.
Can I use an FHA loan on a fixer-upper?
Yes, through the FHA 203(k) renovation loan. We can roll the cost of repairs and upgrades into a single mortgage, with one rate and one closing.
What are the FHA loan limits in Texas?
Most Texas counties cap FHA loans at $524,225 for single-family homes. Higher cost counties go up to $776,250. We confirm your county's exact limit on the call.
Can I get an FHA loan after a bankruptcy?
Yes. Most lenders require 2 years from a Chapter 7 discharge and 12 months of on-time payments after a Chapter 13. Foreclosures and short sales typically require a 3-year wait.
