Is this loan right for you?

Refinancing is right when it pays for itself before you sell or refinance again. Use this checklist.

  • Current rates are at least 0.5% lower than your existing rate
  • You plan to keep the home at least long enough to recoup closing costs (typically 18 to 36 months)
  • You have 5%+ equity for a rate-and-term refi, 20%+ for the best pricing
  • Your credit score has improved since you took the original loan
  • You want to consolidate debt, fund renovations, or remove PMI

Common Questions

What borrowers ask most.

How do I know if refinancing is worth it?
Calculate the break-even point: closing costs divided by monthly savings. If you plan to keep the home longer than that, the refi pays off.
Will refinancing reset my loan to 30 years?
Only if you choose. We can refinance you into a 15, 20, or 25-year term so you do not extend the payoff date.
What does a refinance typically cost?
Closing costs usually run 2% to 4% of the loan amount. On a $300K refi, expect $4K to $8K. These can be rolled into the loan in most cases.
Can I refinance with less than 20% equity?
Yes. Conventional refis are available with as little as 5% equity but PMI applies. FHA Streamline requires no equity at all.
Does refinancing hurt my credit score?
A hard credit pull dings your score by a few points temporarily. Within a few months the on-time payments more than recover it.
