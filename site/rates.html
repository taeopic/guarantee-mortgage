Rate Education

Why rates fluctuate.

Three forces shape what you pay each month. Understanding them helps you time the lock and pick the program.

01

The bond market

Mortgage rates track the 10-year Treasury and mortgage-backed securities, not the Fed's headline rate. When bond yields rise, mortgage rates rise the same day.

02

Your loan profile

Credit score, down payment, occupancy, loan size, and property type all adjust your rate up or down. Two borrowers on the same day get different quotes.

03

The lock window

A 15-day lock is cheaper than a 60-day lock. We help you time the lock so you ride the market in your favor rather than against it.

Lock your rate.

When the market gives you a number you like, we lock it. Once locked, that's your rate through closing, market moves up or down.
