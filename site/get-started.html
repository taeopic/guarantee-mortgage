First Questions

Frequently asked first questions.

The questions almost every Texas borrower asks on their first call. If yours isn't here, ask us directly.

What credit score do I need to buy a home in Texas? +

Conventional loans typically start around a 620 score. FHA can go to 580 with 3.5% down, and in some cases 500 with 10% down. VA and USDA loans often start near 620. We work the full range and will tell you honestly if a wait-and-build-credit plan beats applying today.

How much down payment do I really need? +

Less than most people think. VA and USDA loans allow 0% down for eligible borrowers. FHA requires 3.5%. Conventional starts at 3% for first-time buyers and 5% for repeat. Texas also has down payment assistance programs we can layer on top. We will map your real options at application.

What's the difference between a broker and a bank? +

A bank offers you their products. A broker shops your file across many wholesale lenders and brings back the best fit. We work with 200+ lenders, which means we can usually beat a single-bank quote on rate, fees, or program flexibility. You get more choice, not less.

Will applying hurt my credit score? +

The first conversation does not require a hard pull. When you formally apply, a tri-merge credit report is pulled, which is a single inquiry that counts the same as one regardless of how many lenders we shop within a 45-day window. So shopping is free, credit-wise.

How long does the whole process take? +

Pre-approval can happen in 24 to 48 hours after we have your basic documents. From contract to close on a purchase typically runs 18 to 25 days for conventional, slightly longer for VA and USDA depending on appraisal turn times. Refinance closings often run 21 to 30 days.
