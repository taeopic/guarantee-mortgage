Founder

"A mortgage should fit the family, not the bank."

Dean Kennemer started Guarantee Mortgage in College Station in 2007 with one belief. The biggest house decision a family ever makes deserves a broker who works for the family, who knows the local market, and who picks up the phone when the loan is on the line.

Almost two decades later that has not changed. Dean still personally trains every loan officer at Guarantee Mortgage. He still works files himself. He still answers his cell. Today the brokerage helps Texans buy, refinance, and invest from El Paso to East Texas, but the way the work gets done is the way Dean has always done it. Straight talk, real shopping, on time closings.
