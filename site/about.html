About Guarantee Mortgage

Family-owned. Texas-rooted. Lender-agnostic.

Since 2010, Dean Kennemer and his team have helped Texans buy, refinance, and invest. As a broker, we work for you, not for one bank.

Our Story

Built for Texans by Texans.

Dean Kennemer founded Guarantee Mortgage with a simple idea. The big banks make loans the bank wants, not the ones that fit the family. As an independent broker we shop your loan to over 200 wholesale lenders, which means lower rates, more programs, and fewer surprises at the closing table.

Today our team of 13 licensed loan officers serves clients from El Paso to East Texas, but our heart is still in Aggieland. Our College Station office is our headquarters. Our Mont Belvieu office covers the Houston metro from one of the fastest growing corridors in the country.

We close loans for first-time buyers, veterans, investors, retirees, the self-employed, and everyone in between. We pick up the phone, we tell you the truth, and we get to closing.
